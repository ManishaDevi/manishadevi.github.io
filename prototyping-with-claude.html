Prototyping with Claude: a clinical workspace

The brief A high-fidelity, interactive workspace for clinicians in a multi-specialty hospital, built as a working prototype rather than static screens.

My role

Write the prompt, pick the design system, review accessibility

AI’s role

Building the interactive prototype in Claude

Domain

Healthcare · clinical workflows

Design system: Nord

Before writing the prompt, I picked a design system so the prototype would start from tested components and tokens instead of a look the AI made up.

Why Nord

  • Nord is built by Nordhealth specifically for healthcare software, and it’s widely used in healthcare products.
  • Its principles fit clinical work: quiet by default, dense but not cramped, so clinicians can scan a lot of patient data.
  • Colour is saved for one primary action and for status, so alerts stand out.

Designing to WCAG 2.2 AA

  • Contrast: every text pair meets 4.5:1, and control borders, icons and focus rings meet 3:1. Where Nord Light fell short, I adjusted the colour.
  • Focus: a visible 2px focus ring with a 2px offset on every focusable element.
  • Target size: every target is at least 24px (WCAG 2.5.8).
  • Not colour alone: every status has a word, an icon or both. AI-generated content is labelled as well as styled.
  • Motion: all motion is removed under reduced-motion settings.

The prompt

Prompt

Design a high-fidelity interactive enterprise healthcare application for clinicians working in a multi-specialty hospital.

Problem

Clinicians currently work across fragmented systems to access patient records, medical history, laboratory reports, medication information and clinical notes. This creates cognitive overload and makes it difficult to get a consolidated understanding of a patient's condition.

Goal

The goal is to create a unified clinical workspace that organizes patient information and uses AI to assist clinicians without replacing their clinical judgment.

Primary users

  • Physicians

Core experience

  1. Clinical dashboard
    • Patient list
    • Upcoming consultations
    • Pending lab reports
    • Patients requiring follow-up
    • Prioritized tasks
  2. Patient overview
    • Patient demographics
    • Medical history
    • Allergies
    • Current medications
    • Vital signs
    • Previous consultations
  3. Patient timeline
    • Previous diagnoses
    • Laboratory reports
    • Clinical notes
    • Medication changes
    • Recent hospital visits
  4. AI clinical assistant
    • Generate a summary of the patient's medical history.
    • Highlight relevant changes in laboratory results.
    • Identify missing documentation.
    • Show the source of every AI-generated insight.
    • Allow clinicians to review, edit, accept or reject suggestions.
  5. Clinical documentation
    • Create consultation notes.
    • Review AI-generated drafts.
    • Edit and approve documentation.
    • Maintain an audit trail.
